INGLÊS A BORDO · ÁUDIOS DO MÓDULO 1 NO ELEVENLABS
=================================================

PASSO 1 · CRIE AS 6 VOZES (uma vez só, vale para o curso inteiro)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Menu Voices → Add a new voice → Voice Design.
Cole a descrição (em inglês, funciona melhor), gere, ouça as opções e salve
com o nome do personagem. Use sempre a mesma voz em todas as aulas.
Se uma voz sair "de locutor", gere de novo: queremos gente conversando, não comercial.

ANA
A Brazilian woman in her mid-twenties speaking fluent English with a light, natural
Brazilian Portuguese accent. Warm, friendly and slightly eager, like a new flight
attendant meeting her colleagues. Natural conversational pace, clear diction,
recorded in a quiet room, no reverb.

TOM
A British man in his early thirties from London, standard Southern English accent.
Relaxed, friendly and confident, with a light sense of humour. An experienced flight
attendant chatting with colleagues. Natural conversational pace, clean studio recording.

GRACE
An American woman in her mid-forties, neutral General American accent. Calm, warm and
naturally authoritative, like a cabin manager leading a team. Speaks clearly and at a
steady pace, never rushed. Clean studio recording.

LEILA
An Australian woman in her late twenties with a clear, natural Australian accent.
Upbeat, cheerful and sociable, smiling as she speaks. Natural conversational pace,
clean studio recording.

CAPTAIN
An American man in his early fifties with a deep, calm, reassuring voice and a neutral
General American accent. An airline captain: confident, unhurried, friendly but
professional. Clean studio recording.

MARCO
An Italian man in his late twenties speaking fluent English with a light, natural
Italian accent. Warm, expressive and charming. Natural conversational pace, clean
studio recording.


PASSO 2 · GERE CADA AULA
------------------------
Modelo: Eleven v3 (ou o mais novo que aparecer, como v4). As tags entre [colchetes],
como [warmly] ou [laughs softly], só funcionam no v3/v4. Elas NÃO são lidas em voz alta.

Opção A (se a tela de Text to Speech tiver modo diálogo / "add speaker"):
  crie um bloco por fala, escolha a voz do personagem e cole o texto da fala.

Opção B (Studio, funciona sempre):
  Studio → New project → cole o roteiro da aula SEM os nomes antes dos dois-pontos,
  um parágrafo por fala. Selecione cada parágrafo e escolha a voz do personagem
  (o nome está indicado em cada linha deste arquivo). Gere e exporte em MP3.

Ajustes:
  • Stability: "Natural" (ou entre 40% e 50%). Mais baixo fica expressivo demais.
  • Se uma fala sair estranha, gere só ela de novo.
  • Deixe uma pausa curta entre as falas (o Studio já faz isso).

Salve cada arquivo com o nome indicado (1.1.mp3, 1.2.mp3…) e arraste todos
juntos na galeria de Mídia do painel. Eles entram nas aulas sozinhos.

Números já estão escritos por extenso, do jeito que devem ser falados.
Não troque por algarismos: a voz pode ler "23C" de um jeito estranho.


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.1.mp3 · Introducing yourself · ambiente: sala da tripulação
Vozes: Tom, Ana, Grace
=====================================================================
Tom: [friendly] Hi there! Are you working on the Lisbon flight?
Ana: [warmly] Yes, I am. Hi, I'm Ana... Ana Souza.
Tom: Nice to meet you, Ana. I'm Tom Hughes.
Ana: Nice to meet you too, Tom. Is this your first time on this route?
Tom: [casually] No, I've flown to Lisbon a few times. How about you?
Ana: It's my second time. I joined the company in March.
Grace: [brightly] Good morning, everyone! I'm Grace, your purser for today.
Ana: Good morning, Grace. I'm Ana, and this is Tom.
Grace: Lovely to meet you both. The briefing starts in ten minutes, in room four.
Tom: Great, thank you. See you there.


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.2.mp3 · Small talk with the crew · ambiente: ônibus da tripulação
Vozes: Leila, Ana, Marco
=====================================================================
Leila: [cheerfully] So, Ana, where are you based?
Ana: I'm based in São Paulo, but I'm originally from Recife.
Marco: [impressed] Recife! I've heard the beaches are beautiful.
Ana: [laughs softly] They are. How long have you been flying, Marco?
Marco: About six years now. I started with a small airline in Italy.
Leila: And this is my third year. Do you have any plans for the layover?
Ana: Not yet. Do you know any good places to eat in Lisbon?
Marco: [enthusiastic] There's a great seafood restaurant near the hotel. We could go together.
Ana: [happily] That sounds lovely. Count me in!


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.3.mp3 · Crew roles and positions · ambiente: avião, antes do embarque
Vozes: Grace, Tom, Ana
=====================================================================
Grace: Okay, let's go through the positions. Tom, you're at L one with me, in business class.
Tom: Sounds good.
Grace: Ana, you're at R two. You'll work in the forward economy cabin and the mid galley.
Ana: [carefully] Sorry... R two is the second door on the right, is that correct?
Grace: That's right. And you're also responsible for the duty-free trolley today.
Ana: No problem. Who's the galley operator in the rear?
Grace: Leila. She's at L four. If you need anything, just ask her, or call me on the interphone.
Ana: Perfect. Thanks, Grace.


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.4.mp3 · The pre-flight briefing · ambiente: sala de briefing
Vozes: Grace, Captain, Ana
=====================================================================
Grace: [clearly] Good morning, everyone. This is flight A-U two-one-four to Lisbon. Departure is at eleven forty-five.
Grace: We have two hundred and sixteen passengers: eighteen in business, and one hundred and ninety-eight in economy.
Grace: There are two wheelchair passengers, one unaccompanied minor in seat twenty-three C, and a family with an infant in row thirty.
Captain: [calm, friendly] Good morning, I'm David Reyes, your captain. Flight time is nine hours and ten minutes.
Captain: We're expecting some turbulence about two hours after take-off, so please secure the galleys early.
Grace: Thank you, Captain. Now, a safety question. Ana, where is the nearest fire extinguisher to your door?
Ana: [confidently] It's in the overhead locker above row twelve, on the right-hand side.
Grace: Excellent. Any questions? ... No? Then let's go and have a great flight.


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.5.mp3 · Checking and clarifying · ambiente: galley barulhenta
Vozes: Tom, Ana
(dica: se quiser, adicione um leve ruído de galley ao fundo; mantenha baixo)
=====================================================================
Tom: [a bit rushed] Ana, quick update. We have two more passengers, and one of them needs a vegetarian meal.
Ana: Sorry, could you say that again? It's a bit noisy in here.
Tom: [slower, clearly] Of course. Two extra passengers. One vegetarian meal, for seat seventeen A.
Ana: Did you say seventeen A, or seventy A?
Tom: One-seven A. Seventeen.
Ana: Got it. So, let me check: one vegetarian meal for seventeen A, and boarding starts at eleven?
Tom: Not quite. Boarding has been moved to eleven fifteen.
Ana: Eleven fifteen. Thanks for letting me know.


=====================================================================
ARQUIVO: 1.6.mp3 · Wrapping up and review · ambiente: saindo do avião
Vozes: Grace, Ana, Tom
=====================================================================
Grace: [warmly] Well done, everyone. That was a long flight, but the service went really smoothly.
Ana: Thank you, Grace. You were a great help during the turbulence.
Grace: And you handled the duty-free brilliantly. Your first time on the trolley, right?
Ana: [laughs softly] Yes, it was. I was a bit nervous, but Tom gave me some good tips.
Tom: Happy to help. So... are we still on for dinner tonight?
Ana: Definitely. Shall we meet in the hotel lobby at seven?
Tom: Seven it is. Get some rest first!
Grace: Enjoy your layover, everyone. See you on the flight back.
